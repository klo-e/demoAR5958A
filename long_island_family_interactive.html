Core project purpose

The main promise is protection, not a new lifestyle district.

Long Island is first and foremost a coastal-protection strategy for the low-lying East Coast. The new reservoir and pumping system would also strengthen flood resilience.

What we know

Protection from sea-level rise is a confirmed purpose of the project. The concept includes higher reclaimed land, a reservoir, barrages and pumping stations.

What we do not know yet

The project does not tell you the flood risk or property outcome for a specific home. Detailed design and reclamation profile are still being studied.

👨‍👩‍👧Family takeaway: This is a decades-long protection project. It is not a guarantee about any single building, but it is intended to reduce coastal risk across the East Coast.
